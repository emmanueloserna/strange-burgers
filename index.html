2

Crab Meat

3

Buffalo Cheese

Soft and white, melts like a dream

4

Potato Scraps

Crispy golden bits

5

Spicy-Berry Sauce

Sweet heat in deep magenta

1STEP ONE

BOIL THE WATER

First, boil the water for cooking the crab.

TIP Wait for big bubbles before the crab goes in.
2STEP TWO

ROAST THE BREAD

Roast the Scotland bread and spread the spicy-berry sauce.

TIP Golden outside, soft inside.
3STEP THREE

WATCH THE TEMP!

Don't forget the temperature of the crab.

TIP Not raw, not burnt: right in the green zone.
4STEP FOUR

CHOP & GRILL

Then chop the crab and put it on the grill.

TIP Press the chopped crab into a patty first.
5STEP FIVE

MELT THE CHEESE

While the crab is on the grill, put the buffalo cheese on top so it melts.

TIP Cheese on, then don't touch it.
6STEP SIX

LOAD THE BREAD

Add every ingredient to the bread: the grilled crab with buffalo cheese and the potato scraps.

7FINAL STEP

SAUCE & CLOSE

Finally, add more sauce, assemble and close the Scotland-Krabby Patty.
